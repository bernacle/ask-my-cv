# AI And Learning

## AI And RAG Projects

Bruno built Logfolio, an AI-powered app where users could write daily work logs and generate summaries for performance reviews or tailored cover letters.

He has also been exploring RAG projects like Ask My CV, where career history, resume content, projects, and interview answers can become searchable source material for applications, recruiters, and interview preparation.

Bruno is interested in AI as a product layer, not just as a chatbot. He likes use cases where AI helps retrieve, summarize, structure, or act on useful information.

## Interest In AI Engineering

Bruno is interested in AI engineering because it combines backend systems, product thinking, data, and user experience.

The interesting part is not only calling an LLM API. It is designing the whole system around it: retrieval, prompts, structured outputs, evals, latency, cost, quality, and error handling.

He is especially interested in AI features that are grounded in real workflows and real user data.

## AI Tools In Engineering Workflow

Bruno uses AI tools for brainstorming, debugging, refactoring, writing tests, exploring libraries, improving documentation, and preparing technical plans.

He does not treat AI output as automatically correct. He uses AI as an assistant, but still reviews the code, checks assumptions, and makes the final decisions.

AI helps Bruno move faster, especially with repetitive tasks, alternative approaches, and first drafts of technical ideas.

## Current Learning

Bruno is currently learning more about AI engineering, RAG systems, agents, evals, and how to build useful AI-powered products.

He already knows Go and uses it professionally. He is continuing to deepen his experience with Go, backend systems, distributed systems, identity, and production architecture.

## AI-Related Work Interests

Bruno is interested in AI-related work where he can build real product features, not only prototypes.

Examples include:

- RAG systems over company or user data
- AI agents with tool calls and structured outputs
- Internal workflow automation
- AI-powered search and summarization
- Evaluation systems for LLM features
- Developer tools powered by AI
- Fintech, compliance, or infrastructure workflows enhanced by AI

The ideal AI work for Bruno combines backend engineering, product ownership, and practical AI systems.
