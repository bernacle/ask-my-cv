# Professional Profile

## Engineering Profile

Bruno Simplicio is a senior full-stack engineer, backend-leaning, with 11 years of experience building production software across backend, frontend, APIs, identity, fintech, and compliance systems.

His strongest areas include Go, TypeScript, Node.js, React, PostgreSQL, APIs, microservices, event-driven systems, and product engineering.

He likes owning work end-to-end: understanding the problem, designing the solution, building the backend, connecting the frontend, shipping it, and improving it after real usage.

Bruno usually works best in small or fast-moving teams where engineers are expected to take ownership and think beyond tickets.

## Strongest Work Fit

Bruno's strongest fit is work where he can build real product features, APIs, backend systems, integrations, identity flows, and customer-facing software.

He is not a pure platform engineer. He can work with cloud infrastructure, Kubernetes, Terraform, observability, and production systems when needed, but his strongest fit is product engineering and backend-heavy full-stack work.

He also enjoys AI engineering work, especially RAG, agents, structured outputs, evals, workflow automation, and AI-powered product features.

## Engineering Environment

Bruno does his best work in environments where engineers have ownership, move fast, and stay close to product decisions.

He likes pragmatic, direct, collaborative teams. He enjoys working with people who care about quality without overcomplicating everything. He also values environments where engineers understand the business problem and help shape the solution.

Bruno is comfortable working remotely and internationally. He speaks Portuguese natively and English fluently.

## Problems Bruno Enjoys Solving

Bruno enjoys solving problems where backend systems, product, and real business workflows meet.

Examples include:

- Designing APIs and backend resources
- Building authentication and authorization flows
- Integrating with third-party systems
- Improving reliability of existing workflows
- Designing multi-tenant product models
- Building customer dashboards and internal tools
- Turning ambiguous requirements into clear product features
- Using AI to make workflows faster or smarter

He likes problems that require both technical judgment and product understanding.

## Differentiated Background

Bruno's background combines startup execution, fintech, banking, compliance, identity, APIs, and product engineering.

At Nirvana Labs, he works on a modern cloud product in a small team, building Go APIs, frontend features, authentication flows, WorkOS integration, and customer-facing product surfaces.

At Notabene, he joined as one of the first engineers and helped build Travel Rule infrastructure for crypto compliance, including APIs, dashboards, widgets, integrations, decision rules, and Kafka-based services.

At Banco do Brasil, he worked on Open Finance, secure APIs, OAuth 2.0, developer platforms, and banking systems in a highly regulated environment.

This gives Bruno experience in both fast startup environments and large financial institutions.
