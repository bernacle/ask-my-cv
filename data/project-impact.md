# Project Impact

## Proud Project Work

One project Bruno is proud of is the work he has done at Nirvana Labs around the customer platform, identity, and backend resources.

He worked on core product areas like organizations, projects, memberships, RBAC, dashboard flows, and authentication migration from Auth0 to WorkOS. This work is important because identity and access control are foundational parts of a cloud product. If they are wrong, the whole customer experience suffers.

Bruno is proud of this work because it combines backend design, product thinking, security-sensitive flows, frontend ownership, and real customer impact.

## Hard Technical Projects

One of the hardest projects Bruno worked on was the Auth0 to WorkOS migration at Nirvana Labs.

Authentication migrations are difficult because they touch many parts of the product: login, sessions, users, organizations, enterprise SSO, SCIM, audit logs, dashboard behavior, and API authorization.

The challenge is not just replacing one provider with another. It is making sure the product model, identity model, and customer experience still work correctly during and after the migration.
