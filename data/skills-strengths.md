# Skills And Strengths

## Strongest Technical Skills

Bruno's strongest technical skills include:

- Go
- TypeScript
- Node.js
- React
- Next.js
- PostgreSQL
- REST APIs
- Backend architecture
- Third-party integrations
- Authentication and authorization
- WorkOS, SSO, SCIM, and audit logging
- Kafka and event-driven systems
- Temporal workflows
- Production debugging
- Fintech and compliance systems

## Strongest Soft Skills

Bruno's strongest soft skills are ownership, communication, product thinking, and pragmatism.

He is good at understanding the real problem, asking useful questions, explaining tradeoffs, and helping teams move forward.

He is not the kind of engineer who only waits for perfectly defined tasks. He likes clarifying ambiguity and helping shape the solution.

## Backend-Leaning Full-Stack Orientation

Bruno describes himself as backend-leaning full-stack.

His deepest experience is backend, APIs, integrations, identity, and architecture. He is also comfortable building frontend features and owning the full product flow.

He is not a pure platform engineer. He can work with infrastructure and production systems when needed, but he prefers building product and backend systems.

## Professional Languages

Bruno speaks Portuguese natively and English fluently.

He uses English professionally for work, written communication, technical discussions, and remote collaboration.
